Same-Day Delivery — Booked by 11:30, There by 5

Our core service and the one most of our customers use. Request it before 11:30 AM and it is delivered by 5:00 PM the same working day, anywhere in our three delivery zones. Rates start at $19.

What Same Day actually means

It means a single deadline that you can plan around. If the request reaches us by 11:30 AM, the parcel is in the consignee's hands before 5:00 PM that day. Not “within 24 hours,” not “next business day” — today, before close of business.

That cutoff exists for a practical reason. A driver leaving Brampton at midday can realistically cover a GTA route and still clear the far zones before five. Take the same run at 2 PM and the maths stops working, which is why later requests move to Rush or Direct rather than being quietly accepted and missed.

Who books it

  • Retailers moving stock between GTA stores before the evening trade
  • Manufacturers in Brampton and Milton sending parts to a customer's line
  • Offices getting signed documents across the city the same afternoon
  • Online sellers offering local same-day as a genuine competitive edge

What it costs

Same Day is priced by zone: $19 for Zone 1, $22 for Zone 2 and $32 for Zone 3, or $19 for any trip that stays inside one zone. After the first 50 km add $1 per km. Then $0.15 per pound over 30 lbs, $15 if the job needs a van, and 13% HST. Nothing else.

Our phones open at 10:00 AM. That gives you a ninety-minute window each morning to get a Same Day booked. If that is tight for your operation, submit the request online overnight — it will be waiting for us when we open and we will call you first thing.
