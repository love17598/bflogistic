Three things worth knowing

Same Day only

These are same-day rates. Rush and Direct outside the GTA depend on the hour and where our vehicles already are, so we quote them on the phone rather than publishing a window we might not hold.

Hamilton, Guelph and Waterloo Region are zones now

Hamilton, Kitchener, Waterloo and Cambridge are Zone 3; Guelph and Acton are Zone 2. They are priced from the zone chart — with Rush and Direct available — not from this list.

Buffalo and Montreal

Cross-border and out-of-province runs carry customs paperwork and a different liability profile. We do quote them — just not automatically. Call us and we will price it properly.

Not on the list? The list is not the limit — it is just where we have a standing rate. Tell us where it needs to go and we will price it. Send us the details or ring (905) 500-9443.

Going out of town today?

Same Day out-of-town runs still follow the 11:30 AM cutoff. The earlier we know, the further we can get.
