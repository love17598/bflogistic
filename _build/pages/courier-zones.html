Why the chart is built around Zone 1

We are based at Suite 202-49 Hillcrest Ave in Brampton, and the overwhelming majority of our work either starts or finishes in Zone 1. That is why the rate card reads “for deliveries to and from Zone 1” — the published price is the price for a trip with one end here.

When a job connects two outer zones and never touches Zone 1 — Markham to Burlington, say — we take the higher of the two zone rates and add a $10 inter-zone uplift. Markham is $25 and Burlington is $35, so that run prices at $45 on Same Day. The calculator shows the uplift on its own line so you can see it rather than guess at it.

If a destination is not in the ten zones at all, it falls to the out-of-town rate sheet, which covers more than sixty Ontario towns and cities at a flat same-day price.
