Our Three Delivery Zones

Every city we serve sits in one of three zones, and every zone has one published Same Day price: $19, $22 or $32. The first 50 km are included — after that it is a flat $1 per kilometre.

How the zone price is chosen

Between two zones, the higher zone’s rate applies. Brampton (Zone 1) to Markham (Zone 2) is $22 on Same Day; Toronto (Zone 1) to Hamilton (Zone 3) is $32.

Inside one zone, the price is $19 — whichever zone it is. Ajax to Pickering (both Zone 2) is $19, and so is Kitchener to Waterloo (both Zone 3).

Longer trips add $1 for every kilometre after the first 50. We estimate the distance from the pickup and delivery postal codes, and the calculator shows it as its own line before you book.

If a destination is not in the three zones, it falls to the out-of-town rate sheet, which covers more than fifty Ontario towns and cities at a flat same-day price.
