Rush Courier — Three Hours, Door to Door

Three hours from pickup to delivery, anywhere in the GTA. This is what runs after the 11:30 AM Same Day cutoff has passed, and what you book when the afternoon has gone sideways. Rates start at $28.50.

A three-hour clock, not a three-hour estimate

Rush is measured from the moment we collect. Three hours later it is delivered. Because the window is fixed rather than routed around other work, a Rush job is not consolidated with a driver's existing run — it gets priority on the schedule.

Most Rush bookings are not planned. Something did not ship when it should have, a customer moved a deadline, or a part turned out to be wrong. That is the job this tier does: it absorbs the afternoon that went wrong without anybody having to drive across the city themselves.

When Rush pricing applies

Two ways you end up on Rush rates:

  • You choose it because three hours is the window you need
  • You request a job after 11:30 AM, at which point Same Day is no longer deliverable and Rush becomes the earliest honest option

The second case is worth being blunt about. Plenty of couriers will take a Same Day booking at 2 PM and then miss it. We would rather quote you the tier we can actually deliver.

Rush pricing

Rush is the Same Day base rate × 1.5: $28.50 for Zone 1 or any same-zone trip, $33.00 for Zone 2 and $48.00 for Zone 3. After 50 km add $1 per km (not multiplied). The other surcharges are identical to Same Day: $0.15 per pound over 30 lbs, $15 if a van is needed, then 13% HST.
