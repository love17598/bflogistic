About us

A Brampton Courier That Publishes Its Prices

BF Logistics runs same-day courier and freight services out of Brampton, across ten Greater Toronto Area zones. We are deliberately a GTA specialist rather than a province-wide generalist — it means every destination we serve has a real number attached to it.

What we actually are

Two businesses under one phone number. The courier side moves parcels and documents across the GTA at three defined speeds — same day by 5:00 PM, rush in three hours, direct in ninety minutes — at published zone rates starting at $18. The logistics side handles freight, warehousing and distribution, quoted per job.

We operate white Sprinter vans in black and yellow livery, based at Suite 202-49 Hillcrest Ave in Brampton. If you have seen one on the 410 or in a Mississauga industrial park, that was us.

Why our rates are on the website

Almost no GTA courier publishes a rate chart. The usual reason given is flexibility; the real reason is usually that the number changes depending on who is asking. Ours does not, so there is nothing to hide behind “call us for a quote.”

It also saves everyone time. If our pricing does not suit your operation you will know in thirty seconds instead of after two phone calls, and if it does, you can book without speaking to a salesperson first.

Our fleet carries the same information as this website: the phone number, the domain, and the promise on the rear door — reliable delivery.

How we work

Four commitments we can be held to

Honest cutoffs

Booked by 11:30 AM means delivered by 5:00 PM. Past that, we quote you Rush or Direct rather than accepting a Same Day we cannot make.

Itemised pricing

Base, weight, van, HST. Every quote breaks down into parts you can check against the published chart.

A call back

Every request gets a phone call from a person to confirm it. Thirty minutes during business hours.

No invented extras

No fuel levy, no residential fee, no liftgate charge. If that ever changes, it goes on the pricing page before it goes on an invoice.
