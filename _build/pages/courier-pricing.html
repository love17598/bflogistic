The formula

How a price is put together

Six steps, in this order. The calculator shows you every one of them.

Base rate

Find both postal codes’ zones in the chart above. Different zones: take the higher zone’s rate. Same zone: $19. Multiply by 1.5 for Rush or 1.7 for Direct.

Distance

The first 50 km are included. Every kilometre after that is $1.00.

Weight surcharge

Subtract 30 lbs from the total weight, multiply what is left by $0.15. Under 30 lbs, this is zero.

Van charge

$15.00 if the job needs a van. Otherwise nothing.

Subtotal

Base plus distance plus weight plus van.

HST

13% of the subtotal, added to give the total.

The awkward cases

Where the chart needs a rule

Here is exactly what we do in the cases a zone chart does not answer on its own.

Both ends in the same zone

Ajax to Pickering, or Kitchener to Waterloo, never leaves its zone. Any trip that starts and ends in the same zone is $19 on Same Day — $28.50 Rush, $32.30 Direct — plus $1/km if it runs past 50 km.

How distance is measured

We estimate the driving distance from the pickup and delivery postal codes. It is an estimate, so the calculator shows it as its own line and we confirm it on the call. Rural postal codes cover too wide an area — type the town name instead, or call us.

Rush and Direct out of town

Our out-of-town sheet publishes Same Day rates only. A three-hour or ninety-minute commitment to Sarnia or Barrie depends on the hour and where our vehicles already are, so we quote those on the phone rather than automatically.

Some jobs we will not price automatically. Anything over 500 lbs, more than ten pieces, a destination outside the three zones and the out-of-town list, a trip with both ends out of town, or a run to Buffalo or Montreal. Cross-border work carries customs paperwork and a different liability profile, so it gets a human quote every time.

Delivering outside the GTA?

More than fifty Ontario destinations outside our three zones carry a flat published same-day rate, from Bolton at $40 to Sudbury at $600.
