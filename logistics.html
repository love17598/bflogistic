Capabilities

Six things we do on the freight side

We are a GTA operator, so these are built around Ontario lanes and Ontario warehousing rather than a national network we do not own.

LTL freight

Pallet-level shipments that do not need a whole trailer. You pay for the space you use, and the load moves with other compatible freight on the same lane.

FTL freight

A dedicated trailer for a single consignment. Fewer touches, tighter transit times, and the right answer for high-value or time-critical loads.

Warehousing

Short-term overflow or a standing footprint. Useful when a container lands early, a seasonal peak arrives, or a lease gap needs covering.

Distribution

Multi-stop programmes that run to a schedule — one collection, many drops, the same route every week.

Last-mile

The final leg to the customer's door, run by the same drivers and vehicles that handle our courier work across the ten GTA zones.

Dedicated fleet

Vehicles and drivers assigned to your operation for a fixed period. Your volumes, your schedule, our equipment and compliance.

Inventory into storage, then out again to customers — the whole path in one place.

Why shippers choose BF

Small enough to answer, big enough to move it

The reason our courier customers end up giving us their freight is simple: it is the same phone number and the same people. You are not handed to a different division with a different set of excuses.

We do not publish freight prices, and we are deliberate about that. A pallet of light packaging and a pallet of steel fittings are not the same job, and a “from $X” number on this page would only ever be right by accident. Send us the details and you will have a real number within one business day.

We respond to all freight quotes within one business day. If your enquiry lands after 5:00 PM or over a weekend, the clock starts when we open at 10:00 AM.

What to send us

The more of this you have, the faster and tighter the quote:

  • Origin and destination, with postal codes
  • Total weight in pounds and number of pallets or pieces
  • Dimensions, if anything is oversized
  • Whether either end has a dock or needs a tailgate
  • How often this moves — one-off, weekly, daily
  • Any handling requirements: temperature, hazmat, fragile
